Focus on Durability and Strength

Slide 2

Concrete Grades

Group Grades Characteristic Strength ($f_{ck}$)
Ordinary Concrete M10, M15, M20 10 - 20 $N/mm^2$
Standard Concrete M25 to M60 25 - 60 $N/mm^2$
High Strength Concrete M65 to M100 65 - 100 $N/mm^2$

*Grade suffix represents the characteristic compressive strength of 150mm cube at 28 days.

Slide 3

Design Philosophy

Limit State Method (LSM)

Primary method used. Considers safety (collapse) and serviceability (deflection/cracking).

Working Stress Method (WSM)

Alternative method for specific structures like liquid retaining tanks.

Key Assumption: Tensile strength of concrete is ignored in RCC calculations.

Mild

Moderate

Standard covers are critical to prevent corrosion of reinforcement in aggressive environments.
