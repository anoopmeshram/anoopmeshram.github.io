Motivation / Problem Statement

  • Many leads are generated online, but conversion rates remain low.
  • Companies lack structured approaches to analyze digital intent signals.
  • Sales teams waste time on low-quality leads.
  • Need to identify high-potential customers earlier via micro-actions.
9

Objectives

  • Examine the impact of digital behavior on lead conversion.
  • Identify key intent-based digital micro-actions.
  • Evaluate the role of automation in enhancing sales performance.
11

Research Methodology

  • Type: Descriptive research.
  • Primary Data: Interaction with contractors and builders.
  • Secondary Data: IndiaMART, Justdial, company websites.
  • Lead Tools: Apollo, Map Scraper, Leads Finder.
13

Data Analysis & Results

  • Digital platforms like IndiaMART and Justdial are vital for B2B lead generation.
  • Actions like RFQs and inquiry submissions indicate high purchase intent.
  • Leads with high digital engagement convert more effectively.
  • Classifying leads by intent improves sales efficiency.
14

Conclusion

  • Digital platforms are the critical first touchpoint for buyers.
  • Intent-based micro-actions serve as early buying indicators.
  • Leveraging digital signals allows for better lead qualification.
  • Digital insights improve overall conversion effectiveness.
15

Recommendations

  • Optimize website and active B2B platform profiles.
  • Track customer behaviors like product searches and RFQs.
  • Implement a structured lead management and classification system.
  • Use automation (chatbots/automated replies) for quick responses.
