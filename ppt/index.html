Problem Statement & Motivation

  • B2B companies generate many leads, but conversion remains low.
  • Lack of structured approaches to analyze digital "intent signals."
  • Sales teams waste time on low-quality/unqualified leads.
  • Goal: Use digital engagement micro-actions to identify high-potential customers early.
Source: Slide 108-114

Research Methodology

  • Type: Descriptive Research
  • Lead Generation Tools: Apollo, Map Scraper, Leads Finder
  • Primary Data: Interactions with contractors and builders.
  • Secondary Data: IndiaMART, Justdial, and company websites.
  • Analysis: Excel-based databases for tracking engagement.
Source: Slide 124-129

Results & Conclusion

  • Digital platforms are the primary initial touchpoint in the construction industry.
  • Intent Actions: RFQs and specific product searches indicate a high probability of conversion.
  • Automation (chatbots/automated replies) is recommended to capture leads instantly.
  • timely follow-ups with builders are critical for B2B success.
Source: Slide 146-158
